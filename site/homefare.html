Questions

When does HomeFare open?

Free deal alerts start in early December 2026 for everyone on the waitlist. Paid membership opens in January 2027, and waitlist members get their first year for £29.

Will I be charged for joining the waitlist?

No. The waitlist is free and we don't ask for a card. When membership opens we'll email you, and you choose whether to join. Nothing happens automatically.

What do you do with my email?

We use it only to send you HomeFare deal alerts and updates about your route. We never sell or share it, and every email has a one-click unsubscribe link.

Do you sell flights?

No. We find the deal and send you the details and a link. You book directly with the airline or a trusted booking site, so your ticket is theirs, not ours.

What counts as a deal?

We track each route every day and work out its normal price for that time of year. We only send fares at least 25% below that.

How does the free trial work?

You get 7 days of full membership for £0. We email you when it starts and again before it ends. If you do nothing, you're charged £49 for the year. Cancel any time before then and you pay nothing.

How do I cancel?

Use the "Manage or cancel" link in any of our emails or at the bottom of this page. It takes one click. No forms, no phone calls.

How does the £300 guarantee work?

If your first year of alerts doesn't save you at least £300 on flights you booked, email us before your renewal and we'll refund the £49.

How do I contact you?

Email hello@homefare.co.uk any time. A real person replies within one working day.

How fast do deals go?

Some last weeks, some last hours. Fares can change before you book, which is why we send alerts the moment we find them.

Can you get me cheap flights for Christmas?

December is the most expensive time to fly home. The best way to save is to book early. Our peak-season warnings tell you when Christmas fares start rising.

Which airports do you cover?

London Heathrow, Gatwick and Stansted, Manchester and Birmingham to start, with more UK airports on the way. Tell us yours when you sign up.

My city isn't listed. Can you add it?

Yes. Tell us when you sign up. We add new cities based on what members ask for.
